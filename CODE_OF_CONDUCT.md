# Code of Conduct

Everyone is welcome to take part in Musa CAD, whatever their background or level of
experience. Treat people with respect and help keep this a useful, welcoming place
to ask questions, share work, and disagree.

## Be considerate

- Be patient with people who are learning. Explain things clearly.
- Give feedback about the work, not the person. Listen to feedback on your own work.
- Respect different experiences, opinions, and access needs.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and help put them right.

Harassment, threats, personal attacks, hateful language, unwanted sexual attention,
and sharing someone's private information without permission are not acceptable.
Discrimination based on age, disability, ethnicity, gender, nationality, race,
religion, sexual orientation, or other personal characteristics is not acceptable.
Repeated disruption and unwanted contact after someone asks you to stop are also
not acceptable.

## Where this applies

This code applies to MusaCAD repositories, issues, pull requests, and other project
spaces. It also applies when someone represents the project in public.

## Report a concern

Email [pranay@weberq.in](mailto:pranay@weberq.in) with a description of what happened
and links or other details that would help with a review. Please avoid posting
private conduct reports in public issues. GitHub's **Report content** option can
also be used where it is available.

Reports are handled privately. Information is shared only when needed to review
and respond to the concern. If a report concerns the maintainer, or you prefer
not to contact the project directly, you can [report abuse to GitHub](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).

## How reports are handled

The maintainer reviews reports and considers the context, harm, and any earlier
incidents. Depending on the situation, a response may include:

- Asking someone to stop or correct their behavior.
- A private warning that explains the problem and what needs to change.
- A temporary restriction from project spaces.
- A permanent ban for serious or repeated abuse.

Harmful content may be removed, and contributions that break this code may be
rejected. The reason for a decision will be explained when it is safe to do so.
Anyone affected by a moderation decision can ask for a review by email. Retaliation
against someone who reports a concern or helps with a review is not acceptable.
